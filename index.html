Privacy Policy — 3D Gomoku

Last updated: June 30, 2026

Short version: 3D Gomoku does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline on your device.

None. 3D Gomoku does not collect any personal information or usage data. The app does not require an account, does not ask for any permissions, and does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Third Parties

3D Gomoku contains no third-party advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or social media integrations. No data is shared with any third party.

Children's Privacy

3D Gomoku is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
